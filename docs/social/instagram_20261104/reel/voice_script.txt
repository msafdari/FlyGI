I gave my AI something surprising. A map from a fruit fly's eye. Scientists mapped how its visual cells are arranged. I borrowed that layout to help my program organize what it sees. Then I gave it a puzzle with no instructions. Watch these six clicks. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six. And... level one cleared. Rearrange the map, and the same program took a hundred and ninety-nine actions. Across twenty-five games, the overall results were close. But this puzzle gave me a clue. What else could we borrow from nature?
